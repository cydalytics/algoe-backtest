B

The prediction problem

  • One row is (5-minute bucket T, pool, line, combination). The target is the HK$ that selection takes in [T, T+5).
  • Inputs are only what is known at T: prices posted before T, HKJC's TG / SUP before T, the score, and money history.
  • A row is forecastable only if it was priced before T and its pool is open. Rows that only took money are labels, never inputs.
  • The baseline is last 5 minutes: each selection takes next bucket what it took last bucket.
  • Primary metric: Poisson deviance. Also WAPE per selection, match and active selection, bias, bias on the biggest 1% of matches, Diebold-Mariano against last 5, and days better or worse.
  • Train / serve parity is a test, not an assumption. Training and the live engine build the same bucket, and every model input is compared row by row. It found six real leaks and skews.
C

Turnover: decompose the money, give each piece its own loss

moneys=match total⏟scale×pool share⏟bet-type mix×selection share⏟price, line, side
  • Each level has different drivers. The total answers to popularity, the clock and events. The pool mix answers to phase and score. The selection split answers to price, line position and side.
  • Shares are bounded and stable; only the total carries scale. Each split is scored on its own: misallocated money and share log-loss.
  • 25 pipelines compete: three-stage, four-stage (board total x match share x pool share x selection share) and direct.
  • Estimators: penalised IRLS GLM with a scale-invariant ridge, GAM on P-splines, LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost and an MLP. Every tree and network also has a GLM-anchored twin.
  • The champion has the lowest validation deviance and is reported once on test. A model is promoted only if it beats last 5 and the current champion on the same validation days.
  • The champion is blended toward a hybrid last 5, with the weight chosen on validation days.
D

GLM first, tree second: the anchored models

log⁡μ=log⁡(exposure)+x⊤βˆ⏟GLM, fitted first+ftrees(x)⏟boosted on the GLM’s margin
  • A tree predicts a constant past its last split. A network saturates the same way, because its inputs are clipped to the training range.
  • On a test world with matches 1.6-7x bigger than any in training, plain GBM and MLP forecast 75% too little. The anchored models were within 2-3%.
  • glm_xgb passes the GLM's linear predictor as XGBoost's base_margin. Inside the range the trees learn what the GLM misses: thresholds and interactions.
  • Past the range, the money levels carry the GLM's log-linear trend, capped at 10x the edge value. Every other input is held at its edge.
  • Why not tree first and GLM second: a GLM on top of tree outputs inherits their flat edge. The model that must extrapolate has to be the backbone.
  • The anchored twin is served when it is within 1% on validation deviance and its bias is at most 2 points worse.
  • Exposure enters as an exact offset: LightGBM init_score, XGBoost base_margin, CatBoost baseline. Fitting y / exposure with exposure as weight is exact only for Poisson, not for Tweedie.
E

Features: about 200 inputs on three levels, each tied to a hypothesis

Level Inputs Groups (count)
Match 81 money memory 7, clock bases 28, events 6, game state 11, popularity 5, board and concurrency 8, calendar 9, competitiveness 3, breadth 2, price 2
Pool 56 money memory 5, bet-type family x phase 22, game state 11, events 5, clock 4, popularity 3, price 2, breadth 1, line position 1
Selection 73 money memory 17, price 13, line position 9, bet type 15, game state 11, events 3, clock 2, competitiveness 2, popularity 1
  • Every input has a group, a hypothesis (H1-H27), a fill value and a clip range. Missing, infinite or out-of-range values become the fill or the bound, never NaN.
  • The clock is a set of hinge bases, so a GLM fits a piecewise-linear ramp. Minutes to kick-off use knots at 0, 5, 10, 15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120, 180, 240 and 360.
  • Events are decaying pulses: goal_p3 = ∑e−age/3 over goals, with 10-minute pulses for goals, reds and yellows and a 5-minute pulse for corners.
  • The game state is read against the kick-off favourite, from the sign of SUP before kick-off and never a later value. Features: favourite's lead, underdog in front, reds per side, and tg_shift, the expected goals now minus at kick-off.
  • Price inputs: punter value on HKJC's own belief, odds x p(true) - 1, and on the market median. HKJC's place against the best bookmaker and inside the market range. Odds moves over 5 and 15 minutes, odds against the day's first price, and quote age.
  • A feature-group ablation drops one group, refits and reports the validation deviance it costs.
F

Built for a market that moves

  • Market-index units. Money inputs are divided by the median daily turnover of the 28 cached days that end two days earlier. The match-total model carries the index as exposure, so a growing market never moves the inputs, and no tree extrapolates in HK$.
  • Trailing references, not counts. Board size, pools and lines per match, and lines per pool enter as log ratios to their 28-day medians. Each bet type's margin and money share enter as differences (board_rel, pools_rel, p_margin_rel).
  • Hierarchical empirical-Bayes encoding. League shrinks to global, pool x phase to family x phase to global, and team to its league. Evidence halves every 180 days, and every refit relearns the levels.
  • A match learns within the day. Its own money so far updates a shrunk match effect, so a new league finds its level in hours.
  • The board as a set. Three inputs: a match's share of the board's expected popularity, the gap to the biggest other match, and the board's share in play. This replaces cross-match attention from an earlier network.
  • Exact HK holidays for any year, computed from new moons and solar terms in UTC+8, plus Easter, Ching Ming and the Sunday rules.
G

New bet types, new leagues and lines that move

  • New pool codes are placed by their spelling. An FH, FC or ET prefix gives the segment, and CH or CN means corners. LO is a total, LOH or LOA a team total, DC a handicap, HAD a three-way and CRS a correct score. The code then gets its family's level from day one.
  • New leagues get their parent's level, never an error or a random code. The selftest forecasts an unseen league and an unseen pool code this way.
  • Lines have slots. Rank 0 is the most balanced line. A selection's slot is its offset from that line in quarter goals, by side. Money memory is kept per slot as well as per line id.
  • When 2.5 / 3.5 becomes 1.5 / 2.5 in play, the new line inherits its slot's money (line_new marks it).
  • Office lesson: the slot alone lost to the line's own money on 87 of 87 days. The blend base now uses a line's own last bucket, and its slot only for a new line with no money of its own.
H

Inside the bucket: nowcast and event shift

rˆ=F5m0+xm0+e,restˆ=rˆ(5−e)
  • The live engine updates the bucket forecast F with the money x after e minutes. This is a Gamma-Poisson update in which the forecast counts as m0 minutes of evidence.
  • The rate is capped at 4x the forecast rate. Example: one HK$1M ticket a minute into a HK$50k bucket raises the rest to HK$160k, not HK$1.36M.
  • After a goal, red card or corner at minute k of the bucket, the money follows y=F(w+(1−w)M) with w=k/5.
  • The multiplier M is kept in nested cells: event, clock, whose, favourite strength, score after. Each narrower ratio is shrunk to its parent by 30 pseudo-events, so one red card is counted once.
  • The shift is fitted on validation days and scored on test days.
I

True probability: three GLMs stacked through offsets

  • Each layer is a binomial GLM with the layer below as its offset. Its coefficients are exactly what it adds, and its gain is reported on its own.
  • Trader: HKJC's beliefs, with slopes by phase, price level (the favourite-longshot slope) and bet type, and quote age.
  • Market: the coefficient on the logit gap to the bookmakers' consensus is how far to move toward it. It varies by phase, quote age, time to kick-off and bet type.
  • Customer: weight of money is the logit of a side's share of its line's money, shrunk toward its price, minus its price-implied logit. Plus relative stake size, liquidity and recent price moves.
  • Labels: settled selections only. A quarter line gets the soft outcome (r + 1) / 2, pushes are dropped, and only live prices count.
  • Competitors: every source x raw, Platt, beta, isotonic, logit pool and contextual, chosen on validation log-loss.
  • Money-weighted scores: stake-weighted log-loss and ECE, and expected minus realised margin, because the optimiser's E[GM] is a sum over money.
J

Pricing glue and honest evaluation

  • One score grid per (TG, SUP), collapsed to total and difference marginals. One formula prices whole, half and quarter lines. In play the grid is shifted by the current score.
  • A replication check prices HKJC's own TG / SUP through our pricer and compares it with HKJC's posted board, pool by pool.
  • Demand, not reverse causality. HKJC shortens a price after money arrives, so a share model can learn that a longer price takes less money. The office model showed -0.43 at evens without a market price.
  • So the price response is checked at evens, with and without a market price. If it fails, a fixed elasticity is used, and the log says so.
  • Margin cost by double machine learning. Margin and money are each predicted from the other inputs, cross-fitted by day. The slope of the residuals uses a match-clustered standard error. On a test world the naive slope was -90, DML -8, and the truth -8.
  • Calendar splits only, never random. Drift is PSI month by month. Every bundle carries its feature version and checksum.
