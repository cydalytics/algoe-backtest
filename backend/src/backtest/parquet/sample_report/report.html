2  Is 1 / true odds the right probability?

Read these two sections against their effective sample size. Every 5-min bucket of one selection shares a single settled outcome, so the 14,355 scored rows carry only 252 independent outcomes. Sampling noise alone moves ECE and the realised-vs-expected margin gap by roughly ±3.15%, so treat anything smaller than that as agreement, not as signal.
